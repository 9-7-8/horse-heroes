WALK-THE-CLICK-BUDGET
=====================
Starts on: "walk the click budget", "count the clicks", and whenever a place
           or tab is added, a control is moved in places.ron, or a system
           behind one of the common jobs is built.
Leaves:    docs/click-budget.md with today's counts beside the budgets, and
           a plain statement of any job that is over or not done.

The jobs and their budgets are assets/data/jobs.ron. How the walk works is
docs/click-budget.md. The budgets are the owner's: do not change one to
make a job fit.

STEPS
1. If a job's system has been built since the last walk: take its waits_on
   out of jobs.ron and give it its steps in walk_steps
   (src/dev_screenshot.rs), by pointer and by keys. cargo test fails until
   both are there.

2. Walk. The folder must exist; the run ends by itself after about two
   minutes, on the road.
       New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force screenshots/walk
       $env:IDLE_WALK = "screenshots/walk"
       cargo run -- --seed 6 --mute --window 1120x640 *> run.log
   (cargo by its full path if it is not found: build-and-run.txt.)

3. Read screenshots/walk/counts.txt. For every job, by mouse and by keys:
       took N                   within its budget
       took N, OVER             more presses than its budget
       took N and NOT DONE      the game did not show the job done, or a
                                pointer's press found nothing in sight
       cannot be done yet, #N   it waits on that issue
       NOT WALKED               it is in jobs.ron and not in walk_steps
   Read the pictures beside it: they are where the jobs ended.

4. NOT DONE is a bug or a control that was renamed or given another key:
   find which, and fix it or file it (next-thing.txt, tier 1).

5. OVER is the owner's to rule on, through the question tool: accept it,
   or the screen changes. Until she has, say so in the table.

6. Put the counts and the date in the table of docs/click-budget.md.

REPORT
  The counts that changed since the last walk, and anything over or not
  done. Say that the walk is the game pressing its own controls, not a hand
  on a mouse.

DO NOT
  - Cut a press to meet a number at the cost of the player understanding
    what will happen, or of being able to take it back.
  - Count by reading the code or places.ron. The count is what the running
    game took.
