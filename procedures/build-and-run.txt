BUILD-AND-RUN
=============
Starts on: "run the game", "build it", "does it work", and after any change.
Leaves:    a statement of what was actually checked, and how.

Use the cheapest step that can prove the change:

1. cargo check
   Seconds. Proves it compiles. Enough for a refactor with no behaviour change.

2. cargo test
   For anything in src/sim/ - loot rolls, stat maths, offline progress. A drop
   table is tested by rolling it many times with a fixed seed and asserting on
   the distribution, not by playing until something drops.

3. cargo run
   For anything visible. A Bevy window blocks the shell, so run it in the
   background and read its output from a log file rather than waiting on it.
   Bevy logs warnings and errors to stderr - read them; a window that opens is
   not a clean run.

4. Look at it. For a visual change, take a screenshot and read the image, or
   ask the owner to look. Say which of the two happened.
   Screenshot (src/dev_screenshot.rs): with IDLE_SHOT set, the game saves one
   PNG at 3 s and exits by itself at 5 s, so it can run in the foreground:
       $env:IDLE_SHOT = "screenshots/name.png"; cargo run *> run.log
   IDLE_SHOT_AT sets the second it is taken (default 3). --seed N fixes the
   run; --parts opens the menu parts screen (docs/menu-parts.md), which
   has every gear picture, slot by rarity, at IDLE_SHOT_AT=3;
   --window WxH opens the window at that size in logical pixels (the
   smallest allowed is 1120x640). --cargo N starts with N pieces
   carried, rolled from the seed: the menu tour with --cargo 7 has the
   Inventory's tiles for them in its picture 16. No town leaves by itself (#305), so
   a run with nobody at it stays in town: --keep-going starts it with
   Keep going on, and then the party sets out 12 s after launch, so
   --keep-going at IDLE_SHOT_AT=17 is a picture of the road view in a
   fight. EVERY TIME BELOW THAT IS LATER THAN 12 S IS OF A RUN WITH
   --keep-going (the bursts on the road, the real kills, the deaths, the
   horses walking, --hint at 65.9); the times are as they were. The tour
   and the walk set out by themselves and do not want it.
   --burst throws the loot shower's test burst 1 s after launch (F3 throws
   it at any time) and logs its frame times (docs/loot-shower.md); a
   picture at IDLE_SHOT_AT=2.4 is the burst in the air, at 3.6 its last
   pieces in the air with both beams up, at 5.2 at rest.
   --burst-at N throws it N seconds after launch instead: --burst
   --burst-at 14 at IDLE_SHOT_AT=16.6 is the full fountain on the road.
   --pickup throws the same burst and lets the party collect it (F4):
   at IDLE_SHOT_AT=5.0 it is at rest in the pause, at 6.3 its coins are on
   their way. Real kills, --seed 6: the first enemy falls at 21.3 s (its
   small drop is in the air at 21.6, its coins arriving at 23.85) and the
   final one at 64.5 s (its flash at 64.86, its fountain in the air at
   65.9, its coins arriving at 68.6; it is walking back to town at 71.5,
   the gate coming up the trail, and is in town by 76).
   A death (docs/death-events.md), --seed 6: the first enemy is in
   shards at 21.6 and only its mark is left at 22.3; the final one is
   falling at 64.62, bursts at 64.95, and its shards are going at 66.0,
   with a horse lying exhausted beside it. --seed 3 at 45.6 is an
   exhausted horse walking on; --seed 1 at 72.8 is a lost trail, its
   enemy drawing off.
   The horses (docs/horse-model.md): --bare starts with their hair hidden
   and F5 toggles it. They stand still in a fight (IDLE_SHOT_AT=17) and
   in town (3), beside the town gate; they are walking at 13.5.
   The test burst's flash, on the road: --burst --burst-at 14 at 14.36.
   A flash lasts a third of a second, so a picture of one is a matter of
   a few hundredths.
   cargo check and cargo test do not rebuild the game's own binary: a
   picture taken by running target/debug/horse_heroes.exe directly is of
   the last cargo build or cargo run, so build first.
   The hint (docs/menu-shell.md, "The hint's line"): --hint starts with a
   sample hint showing, about the Journal; F6 shows a sample and takes it
   away, about the next kind of control each time. With --hint --seed 6
   at IDLE_SHOT_AT=65.9 the final fountain is in the air above it.
   Lessons (docs/lessons.md): --seed 6 --keep-going at IDLE_SHOT_AT=17 has
   the first lesson on the hint's line, at 24 the second, at 80 the
   piece's in town. F6 puts the sample over a lesson that is showing.
   IDLE_LESSON walks the first item's lessons by keys (#296): home from
   the first trail it opens the piece's card, selects another horse,
   equips, swaps, and saves a picture of each lesson; then the
   Journal's list of hints, one shown again, and hints turned off in
   Settings (#300), then on again with every place opened, for the
   things to try over the town scene (#303; about 95 s in all):
       $env:IDLE_LESSON = "screenshots/lesson"; cargo run -- --seed 6 *> run.log
   Sound cannot be checked by a picture. --mute plays nothing, for a run
   that is only for a picture. To read what was played, in order:
       $env:RUST_LOG = "info,horse_heroes::fx::sound=debug"
   and look for "sound:" in the log; a missing output device is a Bevy
   warning there. That shows what was played, not how it sounds: only
   someone listening can say that (F3, F4).
   IDLE_TOUR walks the menus instead (docs/menu-shell.md): it presses
   through every place and tab, then the road view and the move between
   the layouts, then a town visit by keys alone, saves a numbered PNG of
   each into the folder it names (create it first), and exits in town
   after the second trail (about two and a half minutes):
       $env:IDLE_TOUR = "screenshots/tour"; cargo run -- --seed 6 *> run.log
   The tour logs "tour: Tab makes N stops, M of them out of sight"; M is
   0, and anything else is a hidden page taking the focus (#244).
   IDLE_WALK counts the presses of the common jobs instead
   (walk-the-click-budget.txt, docs/click-budget.md).
   screenshots/ and *.log are git-ignored. The GOG Galaxy Vulkan-layer ERROR
   line at startup is noise from that overlay, not from the game.

NOTES
  - A throwaway script written outside the repo gets a name nothing else
    has, and is checked to be absent first. %TEMP% keeps other sessions'
    hh_*.py; on 2026-10-10 a write to hh_docs.py was refused, the old file
    of that name ran instead and edited two philosophy files (undone).
  - A long here-document in the Bash tool fails here with "unexpected EOF
    while looking for matching quote" (three times by 2026-10-10, each
    with apostrophes in its text). Write the script with the Write tool,
    under target/, and run that.
  - cargo is not always on the PATH of the shell this tool starts (found
    2026-10-10, in both PowerShell and Git Bash). When "cargo" is not
    recognised, call it by its full path: ~/.cargo/bin/cargo.exe.

  - Do not run cargo fmt on the whole tree: the code is not kept to
    rustfmt's defaults, and on 2026-10-10 it rewrote twelve files that
    had nothing to do with the change (put back with git checkout).

REPORT
  State which steps ran. "Compiles" and "works" are different claims - make
  the one the evidence supports.

DO NOT
  - Pipe a long cargo command through a filter and trust the exit code; the
    pipe reports the filter's. Log to a file and read the real exit code.
  - Leave a game window running at the end of a turn without saying so.
  - Kill anything by name or pattern (CLAUDE.md rule 3). Close the game window
    you launched by the process ID you recorded when you started it.
