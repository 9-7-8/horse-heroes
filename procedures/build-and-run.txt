BUILD-AND-RUN
=============
Starts on: "run the game", "build it", "does it work", and after any change.
Leaves:    a statement of what was actually checked, and how.

Use the cheapest step that can prove the change:

1. cargo check
   Seconds. Proves it compiles. Enough for a refactor with no behaviour change.

2. cargo test
   For anything in src/sim/ - loot rolls, stat maths, offline progress. A drop
   table is tested by rolling it many times with a fixed seed and asserting on
   the distribution, not by playing until something drops.

3. cargo run
   For anything visible. A Bevy window blocks the shell, so run it in the
   background and read its output from a log file rather than waiting on it.
   Bevy logs warnings and errors to stderr - read them; a window that opens is
   not a clean run.

4. Look at it. For a visual change, take a screenshot and read the image, or
   ask the owner to look. Say which of the two happened.
   Screenshot (src/dev_screenshot.rs): with IDLE_SHOT set, the game saves one
   PNG at 3 s and exits by itself at 5 s, so it can run in the foreground:
       $env:IDLE_SHOT = "screenshots/name.png"; cargo run *> run.log
   IDLE_SHOT_AT sets the second it is taken (default 3). --seed N fixes the
   run; --parts opens the menu parts screen (docs/menu-parts.md), which
   has every gear picture, slot by rarity, at IDLE_SHOT_AT=3;
   --window WxH opens the window at that size in logical pixels (the
   smallest allowed is 1120x640). --cargo N starts with N pieces
   carried, rolled from the seed: the menu tour with --cargo 7 has the
   Inventory's tiles for them in its picture 16. A new game starts on
   the road (#292): the party walks out of the gate at launch, with the
   opening's pop-up over the scene (seed 6 at IDLE_SHOT_AT=3), and is
   in its first fight by 5. The scene is not drawn yet at 0.5. --town
   starts in town instead, as a game did before; the tour and the walk
   do so by themselves. No town leaves by itself (#305), so a run with
   nobody at it stops in town after the first trail: --keep-going starts
   it with Keep going on, and then it leaves again 12 s after arriving.
   (Until 2026-10-14 a game started in town and every time below was 12 s
   later, with --keep-going; the times were moved, and two checked.)
   A lesson's pop-up stops the game until it is answered (#353), so with
   nobody at it a run waits at the gate under the opening for good.
   EVERY TIME BELOW IS WITH --no-pause, which shows the lessons and stops
   nothing; the soak never pauses. Without it, seed 6 at any time from 3
   is the opening, the party at the gate (checked at 8, 2026-10-14).
   --burst throws the loot shower's test burst 1 s after launch (F3 throws
   it at any time) and logs its frame times (docs/loot-shower.md); a
   picture at IDLE_SHOT_AT=2.4 is the burst in the air, at 3.6 its last
   pieces in the air with both beams up, at 5.2 at rest. With --town it
   is thrown over the town.
   --burst-at N throws it N seconds after launch instead: --burst
   --burst-at 2 at IDLE_SHOT_AT=4.6 is the full fountain on the road.
   --pickup throws the same burst and lets the party collect it (F4):
   at IDLE_SHOT_AT=5.0 it is at rest in the pause, at 6.3 its coins are on
   their way. Real kills, --seed 6: the first enemy falls at 9.3 s (its
   small drop is in the air at 9.6, its coins arriving at 11.85) and the
   final one at 52.5 s (its flash at 52.86, its fountain in the air at
   53.9, its coins arriving at 56.6; it is walking back to town at 59.5,
   the gate coming up the trail, and is in town by 64).
   A death (docs/death-events.md), --seed 6: the first enemy is in
   shards at 9.6 and only its mark is left at 10.3; the final one is
   falling at 52.62, bursts at 52.95, and its shards are going at 54.0,
   with a horse lying exhausted beside it. --seed 3 at 33.6 is an
   exhausted horse walking on; --seed 1 at 60.8 is a lost trail, its
   enemy drawing off.
   The horses (docs/horse-model.md): --bare starts with their hair hidden
   and F5 toggles it. They stand still in a fight (IDLE_SHOT_AT=5) and
   in town (--town, 3), beside the town gate; they are walking at 1.5.
   --worn starts every horse wearing a piece in each slot (#359): with
   --town --seed 6 at 3 Sable's head is yellow and Morrow's saddle blue,
   each with its pattern and decoration (#361).
   In the lesson walk, 04 has the halter on Sable and 05 on Juniper.
   The test burst's flash, on the road: --burst --burst-at 2 at 2.36.
   A flash lasts a third of a second, so a picture of one is a matter of
   a few hundredths.
   cargo check and cargo test do not rebuild the game's own binary: a
   picture taken by running target/debug/horse_heroes.exe directly is of
   the last cargo build or cargo run, so build first.
   The hint (docs/menu-shell.md, "The hint's pop-up"): --hint starts with a
   sample hint showing, about the Journal; F6 shows a sample and takes it
   away, about the next kind of control each time. With --hint --seed 6
   at IDLE_SHOT_AT=53.9 the final fountain is in the air above it.
   Damage numbers (docs/damage-numbers.md): --seed 6 at
   IDLE_SHOT_AT=5.4 has 6! and then 11! over the first enemy, one after
   the other (#354), and at 6.2 8! and -3 over Morrow (checked
   2026-10-15); at 28 an Elemental Burst's numbers on two enemies.
   The hit poses (#365), --seed 6: at 5.25 Morrow tips forward and the
   toad flinches; at 5.72 the Gloom Sprite leans in, Morrow flinches and
   Juniper tips forward (checked 2026-10-15).
   Lessons (docs/lessons.md): --seed 6 at IDLE_SHOT_AT=3 has the
   opening's line, with no mark; at 5 the first fight's lesson, at 12 the
   abilities', at 18 the numbers', at 24 speed's on Juniper (#355,
   checked 2026-10-15), at 68 the piece's in town. F6 puts the sample over a
   lesson that is showing.
   IDLE_LESSON walks the first item's lessons by keys (#296): home from
   the first trail, walked from the opening, it opens the piece's card, selects another horse,
   opens a second piece in the Inventory (#352), equips, swaps, and saves a picture of each lesson; then the
   Journal's list of hints, one shown again, and hints turned off in
   Settings (#300), then on again with every place opened, for the
   things to try over the town scene (#303; about 95 s in all):
       $env:IDLE_LESSON = "screenshots/lesson"; cargo run -- --seed 6 *> run.log
   Sound cannot be checked by a picture. --mute plays nothing, for a run
   that is only for a picture. To read what was played, in order:
       $env:RUST_LOG = "info,horse_heroes::fx::sound=debug"
   and look for "sound:" in the log; a missing output device is a Bevy
   warning there. That shows what was played, not how it sounds: only
   someone listening can say that (F3, F4).
   IDLE_TOUR walks the menus instead (docs/menu-shell.md): it presses
   through every place and tab, then the road view and the move between
   the layouts, then a town visit by keys alone, saves a numbered PNG of
   each into the folder it names (create it first), and exits in town
   after the second trail (about four and a half minutes on 2026-10-14;
   on the road it answers each lesson with Got it while it waits):
       $env:IDLE_TOUR = "screenshots/tour"; cargo run -- --seed 6 *> run.log
   The card's Details (#228) are the tour's pictures 64 to 66; dragging
   a piece onto a slot and onto a horse, with Bevy's own pointer, 91 to 95
   (89 is the piece lifted into the hand, #363; checked 2026-10-15).
   The tour logs "tour: Tab makes N stops, M of them out of sight"; M is
   0, and anything else is a hidden page taking the focus (#244).
   IDLE_WALK counts the presses of the common jobs instead
   (walk-the-click-budget.txt, docs/click-budget.md).
   screenshots/ and *.log are git-ignored. The GOG Galaxy Vulkan-layer ERROR
   line at startup is noise from that overlay, not from the game.

NOTES
  - A throwaway script written outside the repo gets a name nothing else
    has, and is checked to be absent first. %TEMP% keeps other sessions'
    hh_*.py; on 2026-10-10 a write to hh_docs.py was refused, the old file
    of that name ran instead and edited two philosophy files (undone).
  - A long here-document in the Bash tool fails here with "unexpected EOF
    while looking for matching quote" (three times by 2026-10-10, each
    with apostrophes in its text). Write the script with the Write tool,
    under target/, and run that.
  - cargo was not on the PATH of the shells Claude Code starts (found
    2026-10-10; they run -NoProfile with a PATH older than the user's).
    Since 2026-10-15 .claude/settings.local.json (git-ignored, this
    machine only) sets env.PATH to the Machine and User PATH, which has
    ~/.cargo/bin. If "cargo" is still not recognised, call it by its full
    path, ~/.cargo/bin/cargo.exe; and if a new tool is installed and not
    found, that file's PATH is the one to add it to.

  - Claude Code kills a background command when the machine runs low on
    memory while the session is idle (twice on 2026-10-15, a tour run
    with the Minecraft server up; the same was found on another project).
    A long run that must finish - the tour, the walk, the lesson walk -
    goes in the foreground with a timeout of 600000, which is not reaped.
    Say how long it will take first; ask before anything over 30 minutes.
  - Read the log on disk (run.log, target/*.log) before asking the owner
    what happened. A window that opened, or exit 0, is not a clean run:
    look for ERROR and WARN in it (the GOG line aside).
  - A test that fails after a change may have failed before it: stash the
    change (git stash), run it at HEAD, then decide which.
  - PowerShell traps: `f 'a' + $x` passes several arguments, so wrap a
    computed argument in parentheses; [IO.File] resolves a relative path
    against the process's directory, not the shell's; a CRLF here-string
    never matches an LF file; Measure-Object -Line skips blank lines.
  - An edit containing \uXXXX can arrive as the character itself. Grep
    the file for what went in; a green build does not show it.

  - Do not run cargo fmt on the whole tree: the code is not kept to
    rustfmt's defaults, and on 2026-10-10 it rewrote twelve files that
    had nothing to do with the change (put back with git checkout).

REPORT
  State which steps ran. "Compiles" and "works" are different claims - make
  the one the evidence supports.

DO NOT
  - Pipe a long cargo command through a filter and trust the exit code; the
    pipe reports the filter's. Log to a file and read the real exit code.
  - Leave a game window running at the end of a turn without saying so.
  - Kill anything by name or pattern (CLAUDE.md rule 3). Close the game window
    you launched by the process ID you recorded when you started it.
