    weapon, a mane or tail braid, plus saddlebags and pet saddles.
  - Travel: finite generated world; choose a destination city; distance
    sets travel days; danger from the purity of nearby tiles; a journey
    through danger ends with a boss.

    keep rules, rarity first by default.

    stock travels with the party.

    amount of loot, pay a modest cost, wait to be sent out. No automatic
    retry.

    can be invited back. Residents build with telekinetic horse magic.
  - Breeding: colour-only genetics borrowed from her own mod; aesthetic
    only; background flavour.
