DECIDED
  Hers, through the question tool or stated outright. The full text is in
  vision.md; these are the ones that changed direction.
  - The party follows an itinerary; pillar 2 reworded for it. One world clock
    in days, time away capped at 8 hours.
  - Listing at the stall is the only way to sell. Trade goods sell the same
    way. Larger towns sell faster. Each town has storage.
  - Party starts at three and grows to five. The three starters are expected
    to reach the endgame; retirement is rare.
  - Always a chance of bandits and predators on any road, before and after
    the dragon. Goods trade between towns, with auto-buy, sell floors and
    saved routes; prices change each real-world day within a trend.
  - The world: one hex landmass, party and dragon in opposite corners,
    biomes (speed, forage, predators), towns joined by shortest roads,
    off-road travel. Danger from purity against corruption. A corrupt town is
    liberated by beating the boss at its gate; no cleansing wait.
  - Classic is one finite run and the only setting being balanced. An endless
    mode may come later (#219).
  - No classes and no horse elements. A shared party level, points, one
    shared ability pool, a few loadout slots, free respec in town. Horses
    differ by a stat lean and a trait.
  - Combat: resistance on barding (cap 75%), elemental bonus on weapon and
    braid, element fixed by creature kind, percentage defence, glancing hits
    in place of misses, every hit can crit, one Primer per element, every
    payoff also hits, same-kind bonuses add and different kinds multiply.
